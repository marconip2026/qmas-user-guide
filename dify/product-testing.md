# Product Testing — Request a Quotation (Create Job)

Module: Product Testing
Purpose: create a product testing job and get a quotation from a laboratory.
Start here: https://qmas.icw.io/product-testing → click "Create Job"
Direct link: https://qmas.icw.io/product-testing/create-job
Full guide with screenshots: https://marconip2026.github.io/qmas-user-guide/product-testing/

## Overview
The "Request Quotations" wizard has 4 steps:
1. Product Info: add an existing product or create a new one.
2. Requirements: set the export market, test items, quality requirements and service details.
3. Provider Selection: choose Connected Provider, ICW or TicMart (Marketplace), then click Submit.
4. Done: the Order details page opens. Track the job there or in the Product Testing list.

## Step 1 — Open Product Testing
In the left menu, open Product Testing. The page shows charts (Status, Service Provider, Testing Result) and the list of all jobs. Click "Create Job" above the list, on the right.

![Product Testing list page](https://marconip2026.github.io/qmas-user-guide/product-testing/img/01-list.jpg)

## Step 2 — Product Info
The "Request Quotations" wizard opens. Add the products for this testing order:
- "+ Existing Products" picks from your Product Management list.
- "+ Add Products" creates a new product.

In the "Select Product(s)" pop-up, search by product name or ID, click "Select" on each product, then click "Confirm". You can select more than one product.

![Select Product(s) pop-up](https://marconip2026.github.io/qmas-user-guide/product-testing/img/04-product-selected.jpg)

Check "Item name on report" and "Item no. on report". The lab prints these on the test report. Click "Add PO Information" to link a purchase order (optional). Click "Remove" to take a product off the order. Then click "Next".

![Step 1 with a product added](https://marconip2026.github.io/qmas-user-guide/product-testing/img/05-step1-filled.jpg)

## Step 3 — Requirements
Section 1, Test Requirements:
- Export Market (required): where the product will be sold.
- Test Item (optional): choose a country, then search for test items or citations.
- Quality Requirement: Create New Requirements, Add Existing Requirements, or Import Requirement Template.
- Remarks and Attachment (optional): special instructions and reference files.

![Test Requirements](https://marconip2026.github.io/qmas-user-guide/product-testing/img/07-step2-export-market.jpg)

Section 2, Additional Information:
- Service Level: Regular, Express or Shuttle / Emergency.
- Country of Origin: where the product is made. You can pick more than one.
- Intended Usage: General product, Adult product, Children product or Toys.
- Buyer Name (optional).

"Next" turns blue when all required fields are filled.

![Additional Information](https://marconip2026.github.io/qmas-user-guide/product-testing/img/09-step2-filled.jpg)

## Step 4 — Provider Selection
Choose who should quote this job. You can tick more than one:
- Connected Provider: a lab that is already connected to your QMAS account.
- ICW: ICW finds a lab for you. Pick a preferred lab, or leave "No Preference".
- TicMart (Marketplace): get quotations from labs in the TicMart marketplace.

Click "Submit" to send the request.

![Provider Selection](https://marconip2026.github.io/qmas-user-guide/product-testing/img/11-step3-icw-selected.jpg)

## Step 5 — Done: track your order
After you click "Submit", QMAS shows "Product testing created successfully" and opens the Order details page. The job gets an order number (for example 260008342) and starts as "Under Review".
- The Product Testing Requirements, Products, Supplier, PO and Test Plan tabs show what you submitted.
- "Quotations" on the right shows lab quotes when they arrive.
- The tabs below (Quotes, Failure Records, Reports, Attachments, Invoices, Tag, Activity Log, Message Board) follow the job until the report is ready.

![Order details](https://marconip2026.github.io/qmas-user-guide/product-testing/img/13-order-details.jpg)

## Tips
- Fields marked "(optional)" can be skipped. "Next" stays grey until every required field on the step is filled. A field left empty turns red with a message, for example "Country of origin is required".
- To leave the wizard, click "✕" at the top right.

## FAQ
Q: How do I request a product testing quotation? / How do I create a product testing job?
A: Go to Product Testing, click "Create Job", then follow the 4 steps above. Direct link: https://qmas.icw.io/product-testing/create-job

Q: Where can I see my product testing job after I submit it?
A: QMAS opens the Order details page right away. Later, open Product Testing (https://qmas.icw.io/product-testing) and find the job in the list. A new job starts as "Under Review".

Q: Can I test more than one product in one order?
A: Yes. In Step 1, select several products in the "Select Product(s)" pop-up.

Q: What is the difference between ICW, TicMart and Connected Provider?
A: ICW finds a lab for you. TicMart gets quotes from marketplace labs. Connected Provider sends the job to a lab already linked to your account.
